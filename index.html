Rules for defensible use

  • Housing counts are included in on-campus counts. Never sum those two geographies.
  • Other geographies remain distinct. Public property means the report’s Clery category, not all of Chambersburg.
  • Primary offenses, VAWA offenses, arrests and referrals are separate measures. Do not sum them as unique incidents or people.
  • Zero means no reports in that published cell, not proof no crime occurred. Underreporting and reporting changes affect interpretation.
  • Three years and small counts do not establish a trend or a forecast. No risk scores, heatmaps or patrol allocation recommendations are produced.
  • This snapshot is not automatically refreshed. Police bulletin status may have changed. Check source records.

Exports reproduce the supplied source version. Housing flags, source sections and units travel with the data. External map tiles and Leaflet require an internet connection; tables and exports run locally without a backend.
